I am a backend engineer focused on distributed systems, high-availability services, and production-grade platform engineering.

This site is a focused portfolio. I use it to document real engineering decisions, production trade-offs, incident-driven learnings, and system designs that are relevant to backend, platform, infrastructure, and SRE roles.

## Focus Areas

- Distributed systems and consistency
- High availability and fault tolerance
- Traffic protection and service governance
- Multi-region architecture
- Observability and chaos engineering
- Go services and cloud-native infrastructure

## What I Write Here

I prefer practical engineering write-ups over isolated concept summaries. Each article answers three questions:

1. What was the real production constraint?
2. What options were considered?
3. Why was one design chosen, and what trade-off did it create?
